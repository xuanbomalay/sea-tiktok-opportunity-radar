Evidence before conclusions 研究方法

The limits are part of the analysis.

Read evidence brief ↗
01 / Demand

Compare the same products

Only exact, non-decreasing counters with exact date matches form growth and forecast labels. Rounded displays remain unverified.

02 / Competition

Observe seller diversity

Seller-to-listing ratio and listing-share HHI describe our sample. Low diversity can mean one dominant seller, not an easy market.

03 / Validation

Keep future labels out

Time-based splits purge labels crossing the next split. Baseline, Ridge and gradient boosting compete on validation data.
